# emotales

Corpus of tales with emotional annotations. CC BY 4.0 license.

Please, we ask that users of the corpus, and of the models trained on it, cite our paper: 
Francisco, V., Hervás, R., Peinado, F., & Gervás, P. (2012). EmoTales: creating a corpus of folk tales with emotional annotations. Language Resources and Evaluation, 46(3).
